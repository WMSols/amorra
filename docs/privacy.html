Privacy Policy – Amorra AI

Last updated: March 2026

Amorra AI ("we", "our", or "the app") is an AI companion app for emotional and romantic support. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process:

  • Account information: email address, name, profile photo, and preferences when you register or sign in (e.g. via email or Google Sign-In).
  • Conversation data: messages you send to the AI companion, which are used to provide the service and improve your experience.
  • Subscription and payment data: when you subscribe, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store full payment card details; we receive and store subscription status and related identifiers.
  • Device and usage data: device type, app version, and general usage (e.g. for stability and support).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app, including AI conversations and account management.
  • Process subscriptions and payments via Stripe.
  • Authenticate you (e.g. via Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In).
  • Store your data securely (e.g. using Firebase services such as Firestore and Cloud Storage).
  • Respond to support requests and improve the app.

3. Third-Party Services

We use:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database (Firestore), cloud storage, and related backend services. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Stripe: payment and subscription processing. Their privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.
  • AI / language model providers: your messages may be sent to third-party AI services to generate responses. We use providers that comply with applicable data and privacy standards.

4. Data Retention and Security

We retain your account and conversation data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and comply with law. We use industry-standard measures to protect your data in transit and at rest.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data. You can manage your account and preferences in the app. For requests or questions, contact us via the Support page or the contact details provided there.

6. Children

Amorra AI is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy here and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use our Support page or the contact information provided there.
