Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Open the app and sign up with your email or Google. You must be 18 or older to use Amorra AI.

How do I manage my subscription?
Subscription and payment are handled by Stripe. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the app under your profile or subscription settings, or via the link in your subscription confirmation email.

Is my data private?
Yes. We use industry-standard security and do not sell your personal data. For details, see our Privacy Policy.
